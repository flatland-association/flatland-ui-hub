# Gotthard — sources and what is derived

Generated by `backend/scripts/generate_gotthard.py` (layer 1 of
`docs/plans/gotthard-scenario.md`). Neutral data: no grid, no step length.
Re-run the script when a source or the scope changes; do not edit the JSON by hand.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `gotthard.topology.json` | Operating points, sections with km, the base-tunnel tubes, the tube-change points |
| `gotthard.traffic.json` | Passenger trips of one service day (timetable), synthetic freight, and the volumes behind it |

## Sources

| What | Source | Retrieved |
|---|---|---|
| Operating points and km (mountain line, base tunnel) | SBB open data, [`linie-mit-betriebspunkten`](https://data.sbb.ch/explore/dataset/linie-mit-betriebspunkten/), lines 600, 594, 595 | 2026-10-04 |
| Annual train counts per section, direction and business | SBB open data, [`zugzahlen`](https://data.sbb.ch/explore/dataset/zugzahlen/), year 2025 | 2026-10-04 |
| Passenger timetable | Swiss GTFS, `gtfs_fp2026_20260930`, [opentransportdata.swiss](https://opentransportdata.swiss/en/dataset/timetable-2026-gtfs2020), service day 2026-10-06 (Tuesday) | 2026-10-04 |

## Licence — to be confirmed before this is published

- **opentransportdata.swiss (GTFS):** attribution is required ("the URL
  opentransportdata.swiss must be cited as the source"), commercial use and
  derived data are allowed, derived data is published under the user's name.
  Read from the platform's terms of use on 2026-10-04.
- **SBB open data:** both datasets point to <https://data.sbb.ch/page/licence>.
  That page could not be read automatically (script-rendered), so the exact
  terms are **not verified**. The repository is public. A human has to read the
  page and confirm attribution and redistribution before the next release.

## What is derived or assumed

- **Section lengths** are km differences on the SBB line (basis `derived`).
  The west tube is assumed to be as long as the east tube (the data has its
  km, but the plan models one length per section).
- **Track counts** on the mountain line are assumed double (`tracksBasis:
  assumed`). Track layout inside stations (switches, loops, platform count) is
  **not in the data**.
- **Pozzo Negro → Pollegio Nord** is a real connector in the train counts
  (5806 freight trains a year), but SBB gives no length for it. The length is
  the straight-line distance from the coordinates, so it is a lower bound.
- **Speeds** are typical values (`speedAssumptions`), not data.
- **Passenger route** (`BG` or `GBT`) is inferred: a trip that calls at a
  station found only on the mountain line is `BG`, otherwise `GBT`.
- **Freight is synthetic.** The volume is the published 2025 annual count
  divided by 365 per direction. The times are spread evenly over the day with a
  seeded jitter, because the path catalogue is not public. In 2025 almost all
  freight uses the base tunnel and about one train a day per direction uses the
  mountain line.
- `in_richtung` in `zugzahlen` does not say which direction is north, so the
  two directions are treated as equal.
