# Experiment design canvas

One sheet per group, for Part 4 of the workshop. Fill it in before you build.
Short answers are enough.

**Group:** ________________  **Members:** ________________________________

## 1. Question

What do you want to find out about humans working with an AI? One sentence.

> Example: Do operators follow a recommendation more often when the AI shows
> how uncertain it is?

&nbsp;

## 2. What you compare

The one thing that differs between your two conditions. Everything else
stays the same.

| | Condition 1 | Condition 2 |
|---|---|---|
| What differs | | |

What kind of change is it? Tick one.

- [ ] Interaction mode (Recommendation, Co-Learning, Director)
- [ ] Interface (a widget added, removed or placed elsewhere)
- [ ] Who dispatches (human, synthetic dispatcher, human with a shift partner)
- [ ] Scenario (network, incident, traffic)
